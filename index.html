About

A senior engineer, not a template shop.

I've spent 5.5 years building production frontends where mistakes were expensive.

A bad deploy meant a bank's customers couldn't reach their rewards, or millions of Verizon users hit a broken page. That taught me to ship with tests, accessibility, and a rollback plan by default.

Now I bring that same rigour to freelance and contract work — for teams who want their web app built properly, their slow frontend fixed, or real AI features added without it turning into a science project. You get an engineer who has done this at scale and cares whether the thing actually works in production.
